Project S.P.H.E.R.E. — Spatial Positioning Harmonic Empirical Resonance Experiment

Mission

To test, openly and rigorously, whether location alone affects the measured resonance of a controlled physical object, and to publish the outcome whatever it is.

Scope (founding phase)

Precision resonance metrology of passive copper artifacts at fixed positions.

Out of scope: teleportation claims, human-subject studies, medical applications, and token or financial products.

Standards

  • Preregistration of the protocol and analysis plan before any confirmatory data is collected.
  • Blinding and randomization of measurement positions.
  • Controls and null tests, including sham moves, label swaps, rotations, and dummy objects.
  • Independent review of the protocol before data collection.
  • Open data and code: raw data is kept unaltered, and every result can be reproduced from raw data plus published code.
  • Independent replication before any positive claim is made.

Stage gates

GateRequirement to pass
G0 — Packet adoptedFounding packet reviewed, signed, and published
G1 — Protocol reviewedAt least 2 independent reviewers plus a statistician sign off on the first experiment protocol
G2 — Instrument qualifiedCalibration shows the noise floor and thermal coefficient are good enough to detect the smallest effect of interest
G3 — PreregisteredProtocol and analysis plan frozen on the Open Science Framework (OSF)
G4 — Pilot completeBlinded runs complete, analysis run exactly as preregistered, results published
G5 — ReplicatedAn independent team repeats the protocol; combined results published

Publication commitment

Results are posted on spheredesci.org and an open preprint server within 60 days of unblinding, whatever the outcome. Null and inconclusive results are published with the same prominence as positive ones.

Research integrity

  • Every protocol deviation is logged publicly with a date and reason.
  • Conflicts of interest and funding sources are disclosed.
  • Funders and sponsors have no say over results or publication.
  • No medical, wellness, or financial claims are made.

Amendment

Changes to this charter are versioned in the project's GitHub repository and noted in the public changelog.
