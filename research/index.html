Synchronized Measurement Research Hub

A proposed expansion of the measurement process: synchronized four-object acquisition, Earth-motion questions, related research commentary, hardware guidance and public design review.

Added October 9, 2026 · Proposal stage · Draft for public review

Status: proposal stage. No experiment results, community opinions, installed-hardware claims or production submissions. These additions do not amend the current draft LOC-1 protocol.

Where this fits

The project’s first experiment, LOC-1, compares a moved test sphere with a fixed reference. The pages here examine whether measuring several objects in the same time window would make that test more diagnostic, and what it would cost in calibration. They are proposals for review; the LOC-1 draft remains the reference design. For instruments and budget, see the equipment procurement plan and budget and transparency.
