Decentralized science, often shortened to DeSci, is an umbrella term for efforts to distribute parts of research funding, review, governance, or collaboration more openly. The term covers different proposals rather than one technical system.

## Openness and accountability

Publishing protocols, sharing analysis code, documenting funding, and inviting independent criticism can make research easier to inspect. Those practices do not establish that a hypothesis is correct or that a study's design is sound.

## Not a synonym for blockchain

Some projects use blockchain tools; others rely on conventional publications, repositories, or community processes. Open science practices predate blockchain technology and do not require tokens, coins, or on-chain records.
