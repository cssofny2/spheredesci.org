Equipment Procurement & Utilization Plan

This planning document defines a staged, measurement-first instrument stack for Project S.P.H.E.R.E.’s proposed locational-variable resonance experiment. The protocol is explicitly falsifiable: the equipment is intended to characterize resonance and environmental confounders, not to assume a non-standard physical effect exists. All claimed signals require blinded controls, calibration traceability, independent replication, and public raw-data review.

Planning scope

Six core infrastructure categories

Pricing basis

Indicative USD; request formal quotes

Acquisition principle

Calibrated capability before scale

Read before procurement

Vendor configurations, calibration status, included options, freight, installation, annual service, chamber/radiated-emissions compliance, and facility power can materially change total cost. “Used” figures are market indicators, not offers. Do not purchase an instrument without confirming operating range, accessories, software licenses, NIST-traceable calibration status, serviceability, export restrictions, and local safety requirements.

01 · PRIMARY MEASUREMENT

3D Scanning Laser Doppler Vibrometer

Primary selection: Polytec PSV-500-3D scanning vibrometer system. Polytec laser Doppler vibrometers are non-contact precision vibration sensors; legacy PSV-500 documentation describes full-field scans with a 50° × 40° scan angle, while configured 3D systems are typically quotation-based.

Vendor, model, specifications & pricing

  • Vendor / make / model: Polytec GmbH / PSV-500-3D.
  • Key specifications: Non-contact, full-field vibration measurement; 3D vector measurement using three sensor heads; legacy PSV documentation cites up to 25 MHz bandwidth and 30× optical zoom. Confirm exact configuration with Polytec.
  • Manual / brochure: Polytec vibrometry product documentation.
  • New: quotation-only; planning allowance $300,000–$500,000 configured.
  • Used/refurbished: planning allowance $75,000–$250,000, configuration- and calibration-dependent.

Purpose, experiment role & utilization

  • Typical use: Non-destructive modal analysis, acoustic/noise-vibration testing, structural dynamics, MEMS and product R&D.
  • Experiment use: Map resonance modes of matched copper spheres and extract repeatable modal-frequency changes while correlating environmental logs and controls.
  • Alternatives: Polytec PSV-500-Xtra; Polytec PSV-400-3D (legacy/used-market option).
  • Income opportunities: Paid modal-analysis and ODS scans for manufacturers; university/core-facility time; acoustics/NVH consulting; paid training workshops; anonymized benchmark datasets and grant-supported collaborations.

02 · RF CHARACTERIZATION

Vector Network Analyzer

Primary selection: Keysight E5080B ENA. It is available in 2- and 4-port configurations spanning 9 kHz to 53 GHz, with up to 140 dB dynamic range depending on configuration.

Vendor, model, specifications & pricing

  • Vendor / make / model: Keysight Technologies / E5080B ENA.
  • Key specifications: 2- or 4-port; 9 kHz to 4.5/6.5/9/14/20 GHz standard configurations and selected configurations to 53 GHz; up to 140 dB dynamic range; optional enhanced TDR.
  • Manual / support: Keysight E5080B technical support and manuals.
  • New: listed suggested price around $50,981 for a base configuration; planning range $50,000–$120,000 with options.
  • Used/refurbished: planning range $20,000–$70,000; Keysight states used equipment can save 30%–70%, depending on model and condition.

Purpose, experiment role & utilization

  • Typical use: Characterize S-parameters, impedance, resonators, antennas, filters, cables, and RF/microwave components.
  • Experiment use: Measure transfer functions, Q factor, impedance, and RF coupling of fixtures and shielding to distinguish hardware/RF artifacts from candidate resonant effects.
  • Alternatives: Keysight E5071C ENA (lower-cost used option); Rohde & Schwarz ZNB series.
  • Income opportunities: RF component characterization; antenna/cable verification; fixture validation; short-term instrument rental with trained operator; EMC/RF test services and paid technical training.

03 · ELECTROMAGNETIC CONTROL

RF Shielded Enclosure

Primary selection: ETS-Lindgren Series 81 modular RF shielded enclosure, sized after facility and protocol engineering. A shielded room is an engineered system, not a portable box: doors, penetrations, filters, grounding, ventilation, fire protection, and verification are integral.

Vendor, model, specifications & pricing

  • Vendor / make / model: ETS-Lindgren / Series 81 shielded enclosure.
  • Key specifications: Modular shielded panels; engineered room size, door, filtered penetrations and optional absorber determined by design. Example installed copper enclosure dimensions cited by ETS-Lindgren: 4.2 m × 7.9 m × 3.9 m.
  • Manual / documentation: ETS-Lindgren manuals library.
  • New: quotation-only; planning range $100,000–$400,000+ installed.
  • Used/refurbished: planning range $25,000–$150,000 before disassembly, freight, redesign, installation and revalidation.

Purpose, experiment role & utilization

  • Typical use: EMC/EMI testing, sensitive RF work, secure communications and electromagnetic isolation.
  • Experiment use: Create documented RF controls and run A/B measurements with verified shielding performance; it cannot by itself eliminate every confounder.
  • Alternatives: ETS-Lindgren Series 82; Frankonia shielded room / anechoic-room configuration.
  • Income opportunities: Pre-compliance EMC screening; RF quiet-room rental; antenna/device prototype testing; university/industry collaborations; secure-device testing, subject to proper accreditation and safety controls.

04 · ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL

Temperature & Humidity Test Chamber

Primary selection: ESPEC SH-series or equivalent reach-in temperature/humidity chamber. Final chamber volume, feedthroughs, vibration isolation and acoustic behavior must be validated for the actual specimen fixture.

Vendor, model, specifications & pricing

  • Vendor / make / model: ESPEC / SH-series temperature & humidity chamber.
  • Key specifications: Model-dependent. Representative modern environmental chamber capability can span −70 °C to +150 °C and 20%–98% RH; confirm uniformity, stability, heat load, porting and calibration for selected model.
  • Manual / product documentation: ESPEC humidity chamber documentation.
  • New: planning range $30,000–$100,000+; basic standard test chambers may start around $14,000–$20,000.
  • Used/refurbished: planning range $8,000–$50,000, excluding recommissioning and calibration.

Purpose, experiment role & utilization

  • Typical use: Environmental reliability, stability, shelf-life and material/product qualification testing.
  • Experiment use: Quantify and bound frequency shifts due to temperature and humidity, then test only within defined, logged environmental limits.
  • Alternatives: Weiss Technik reach-in chambers; Binder climate chambers.
  • Income opportunities: Environmental stress screening; electronics and product reliability testing; small-batch qualification services; temperature/RH data logging programs; educational lab rental.

05 · MECHANICAL NOISE CONTROL

Vibration-Isolated Optical Table

Primary selection: Newport RS4000-series optical table with pneumatic isolation, selected after a site-vibration survey and load-layout design. The table does not replace site characterization, structural isolation, or vibration logging.

Vendor, model, specifications & pricing

  • Vendor / make / model: Newport / RS4000 series with compatible isolation supports.
  • Key specifications: Honeycomb optical-table platform; model-specific working surface, thickness, damping and load capacity. Specify dimensions, broadband damping and pneumatic isolation for the vibrometer/fixture load.
  • Manual / product documentation: Newport RS4000 optical-table information.
  • New: planning range $15,000–$50,000 with supports and accessories.
  • Used/refurbished: planning range $3,000–$20,000 plus rigging, inspection and new isolator service.

Purpose, experiment role & utilization

  • Typical use: Stable optical alignment, microscopy, interferometry, photonics and precision metrology.
  • Experiment use: Reduce and measure mechanical coupling into the sphere fixture and vibrometer optical path; compare isolated and deliberately perturbed control runs.
  • Alternatives: TMC CleanTop optical table; Thorlabs Nexus optical table and isolation system.
  • Income opportunities: Photonics/microscopy bench rental; optical-alignment training; prototype assembly services; paid test-fixture integration and metrology demonstrations.

06 · ELECTRICAL TRACEABILITY

Electrical Power Quality Calibrator

Primary selection: Fluke Calibration 6105A Electrical Power Quality Calibrator, with reference-clock and output modules chosen for the instrument fleet. It supplies pure sinusoidal voltage to 1008 V and current to 21 A; it is specialized calibration infrastructure rather than a substitute for a general-purpose waveform generator.

Vendor, model, specifications & pricing

  • Vendor / make / model: Fluke Calibration / 6105A.
  • Key specifications: Pure sinusoidal voltage to 1008 V; current to 21 A; up to 50 VA from voltage terminals; optional modules/configurations. Confirm traceability and uncertainty requirements.
  • Manual / product documentation: Fluke 6105A / 6100B product documentation.
  • New: quotation-only; planning range $80,000–$130,000 configured.
  • Used/refurbished: observed-market reference $71,880 for a used 6105A; planning range $45,000–$80,000 depending on options and calibration.

Purpose, experiment role & utilization

  • Typical use: Calibrate power-quality analyzers, wattmeters, energy meters and related electrical test instruments.
  • Experiment use: Validate electrical measurement-chain accuracy and provide traceable stimulus/checks for power-quality instrumentation used to monitor facility and fixture power conditions.
  • Alternatives: Fluke 6100B; Transmille 3000-series multifunction calibrator (verify applicable power-quality capability).
  • Income opportunities: Meter and power-analyzer calibration; electrical quality audits; metrology training; instrument-validation contracts; regulated-industry partnerships, within accredited-scope limits.

Phased acquisition plan

Phase 1 — Baseline

Acquire site/environment sensors, specimen fixtures, calibrated VNA access, and a used optical-isolation platform. Establish null tests and publish protocols before large capital purchases.

Phase 2 — Controlled measurement

Add environmental chamber access and contracted/leased LDV time. Demonstrate repeatability, uncertainty budgets, blinded analysis, and independently reproducible negative or positive results.

Phase 3 — Core facility

Purchase the LDV and engineered shielded enclosure only if Phase 2 evidence justifies them and the revenue plan supports maintenance, calibration, insurance, and trained staffing.
