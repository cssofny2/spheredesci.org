What is S.P.H.E.R.E.?

Key facts

  • Project S.P.H.E.R.E. is an open, independent research initiative proposing a controlled test of a locational-variable hypothesis.
  • The proposed test uses a hollow copper sphere and one mechanical resonance above 230 kHz; this band is a proposal, not a validated threshold.
  • Nothing here is a result. Claims are framed as hypotheses, plans and drafts until independent review and replication exist.

S.P.H.E.R.E. (Spatial Positioning Harmonic Empirical Resonance Experiment) is an open, independent research project asking a simple question with a careful method: if you move an object, and change nothing else, does its natural "ring" — its resonant frequency — change?

Mainstream physics says it should not, once temperature, vibration, and other ordinary effects are accounted for. An idea from the Bashar material, a body of channeled teachings, suggests that location may be part of an object's own vibrational makeup. We think that question is worth testing properly. Not because we assume the idea is true, but because it makes a prediction that can be measured.

What we are doing

We are building a small, shielded, temperature-controlled measurement setup around a precision copper sphere. We will measure its resonance with laser and electromagnetic instruments locked to an atomic-grade clock, and move it between positions on a randomized, blinded schedule. Before collecting any data, we will publish exactly how we will analyze it and what result would count as "nothing found."

What we are not claiming

We have no results yet. Nothing on this site is a scientific finding until it has been measured, reviewed, and reproduced. We are not claiming teleportation, new physics, or any health, spiritual, or financial benefit. If we find no effect, we will publish that just as prominently as we would publish a positive result.
