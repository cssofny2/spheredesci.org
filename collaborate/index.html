Community / Participate

Build a stronger experiment, not a stronger claim.

We need honest feedback, careful checks, and teams who can repeat the test. You do not need to agree with our hypothesis. We are asking for interest only; there are no funded jobs or named partners.

Updated October 9, 2026 · Proposal stage

Independent reviewers

We seek reviewers in physics, careful measurement, radio signals, and statistics. Can LOC-1 tell a real position effect from changes in the room or tools?

  • Challenge what we plan to measure and the frequency range.
  • Review the error list, room sensors, and mounting plan.
  • Check blind analysis, separate readings, effect limits, sample size, and stop rules.
  • Report financial or research conflicts before review.

We will not share names or endorsements without permission. No review fees are funded or promised.

Community design review New · Oct 9, 2026

Useful feedback explains what works, what fails, and why. Review the four-object test plan. Is reading all four objects at once worth the extra checks? How can we check timing? What else might look like a position effect?

Read the community review prompts, proposed protocol, and error budget. No responses have been collected or published.

Instrument access and replication partners

Support might include time with a laser vibration meter, a timing clock, room sensors, fixture building, or technical advice. We must test the equipment we use, not just trust the maker's best-case claims.

We seek teams to follow a dated test plan, collect data on their own, and publish every outcome: positive, no effect, or unclear. No team has agreed to repeat the test yet.

Documentation and software contributors

Post a clear issue in the website repository. Good first tasks include accessibility checks, clearer writing, source fixes, and ways to organize data.

Before large research tasks, paid work, or new data software starts, agree on the work and who owns it. An author must do key work, review the final paper, and take responsibility for it. A grant or donation alone does not make someone an author.

Introduce yourself

Use the interest form and choose the closest role. Say what you know, what equipment or review help you can offer, and any limits on your time or work.

The form sends messages through FormSubmit to contact@spheredesci.org. Read the privacy notice first. Do not send passwords, private health data, unpublished work, or wallet secrets.
