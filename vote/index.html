Community votes.

Help choose the project's public identity. Each vote is a separate project with its own items and results.

Added October 16, 2026 · Proposal stage

Informal polls. Votes here collect community preferences about the project's identity and public materials. They are not research and do not measure or validate anything about the experiment.

Open votes

Each vote is its own project with its own items, ballots and results.

Brand · 109 items

Logo concepts

109 logo concepts from six design rounds. Keep or drop each one, rate it 1 to 10, leave a note, or remix a mark with a text prompt and enter it into the vote.

/vote/logo-concepts

How votes work

  • Rate each item from 1 to 10 and choose Keep or Drop. Comments are optional.
  • You get one ballot per item. A ballot is locked once submitted.
  • Results show the mean rating, the share of Keep votes and the number of ballots.
  • Some projects let you remix an item with a text prompt and add it to the pool.
