Vote on the S.P.H.E.R.E. logo concepts.

Keep or drop each concept, rate it from 1 to 10, leave a note, or remix a mark with a text prompt. The marks are design proposals for the project identity.

Added October 16, 2026 · Proposal stage

This is an informal community poll. It collects design preferences for the project identity. It is not research, a measurement or a scientific result, and the outcome does not endorse any claim about the experiment.

Round one 18 logos

Eighteen broad directions: standing waves, Chladni patterns, copper shells, scan lines and a type-only wordmark.

Round two 19 logos

Round three 18 logos

Hybrids 18 logos

Set seven 18 logos

Wave-dot fields, faceted forms and multi-sphere arrangements.

How voting works

  • Keep or Drop and a rating from 1 to 10 are required. A comment is optional, public, and shown only on the Results tab.
  • You get one ballot per logo. A ballot is locked when you submit it.
  • The ranking uses the mean rating. Logos with fewer than three ballots are marked provisional.
  • In the Remix lab, you describe a change to a mark, review the result, and submit it. It joins the pool as a new entry; you cannot vote on your own remix.

What the poll can and cannot show

Each browser gets a random anonymous voter ID stored in a cookie, so one browser casts one ballot per logo. Anyone who clears cookies or uses another device could vote again, so treat the results as a popularity signal, not an audited count. Daily limits per network and per voter reduce abuse. Comments and remix names that contain links are rejected.

Privacy

The site stores your ballots, comments and remixes with a random voter ID. It stores a salted hash of your network address for daily abuse limits, not the address itself. It does not ask for your name or email. See the privacy notice.

About these concepts

The 109 marks come from six design rounds for the project identity. They are drawn in SVG, so each one can be opened and reused at any size. They are design proposals only. Nothing about a logo changes the research protocol or any claim on this site.
