# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Researchers and prospective collaborators looking for clear, technically useful
descriptions of the project's proposed research, methods, and scientific
concepts.

## Product Purpose

Project S.P.H.E.R.E. publishes an open proposal for experimental research and
makes its methods, plans, and educational context accessible to readers.

## Positioning

The project distinguishes hypotheses, proposed methods, observations, and
findings, and presents its research as a proposal until real records exist.

## Operating Context

Readers use the public static website to review research articles, background
concepts, terminology, protocols, and project information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public site is static HTML deployed through Cloudflare Workers assets.
- MDX content is rendered to static HTML during the build; no client-side
  application framework is required.
- First-party analytics uses daily aggregate counts without cookies or stored
  visitor IP addresses.
- Published research must remain accurate about the project's proposal stage.

## Brand Commitments

Use the organization name Project S.P.H.E.R.E., the official site address
`https://spheredesci.org`, and the existing brand palette and assets.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current project overview, draft protocol,
proposal-stage research pages, educational explainers, and brand assets. It
contains no measurement results.

## Product Principles

- Keep hypotheses distinct from findings.
- Make research methods and context easy to inspect.
- Preserve accessible reading and navigation without JavaScript.
- Collect only aggregate analytics needed to understand content use.
