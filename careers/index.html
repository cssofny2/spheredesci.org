Roles we plan to fill first.

S.P.H.E.R.E. is at proposal stage. The roles below are the initial team we have budgeted for. They are contingent on funding, and nothing here is a job offer or a promise of payment.

Updated October 9, 2026 · Budget targets, not commitments

Hiring status

We are collecting expressions of interest. Work can begin only after funding arrives and the board approves each role. Pay is a first-year estimate for a lean $400,000 launch plan. It is below market because roles are part time or short term and the project is early. Amounts may change.

Founder role

The project founder holds this role. It covers planning, fundraising, the website, cloud services, security, open data, and outside workers. The founder does not approve the science; the science lead does. The founder's pay is a conflict of interest. Board members with no stake in the decision must approve it and keep a record. The project must keep at least six months of planned operating funds.

Planned hiring order

  1. Days 1 to 30: recruit the science lead and measurement engineer.
  2. Days 15 to 45: find a lawyer, bookkeeper, and grants lead.
  3. Before hiring a lab: lab operations coordinator.
  4. Before data collection: an outside statistician must write and date the analysis plan.
  5. With more funding: consider full-time roles.

Services procured, not hired

Legal help, company setup, software security reviews, and a host university or commercial lab would be paid services, not staff roles. Advisory board members would start as volunteers. Approved expenses may be repaid.

Independence and conflicts

Reviewers must be separate from the people running the experiment. Applicants must report money or research ties. Pay will not depend on a positive result. Contributions or pay do not buy equity, tokens, royalties, or a financial return.
