Roles we plan to fill first.

S.P.H.E.R.E. is at proposal stage. The roles below are the initial team we have budgeted for. They are contingent on funding, and nothing here is a job offer or a promise of payment.

Updated October 9, 2026 · Budget targets, not commitments

Hiring status

We are collecting expressions of interest now. Engagements begin only after the relevant funding is received and the board approves the scope. Pay figures are first-year budget targets under a lean $400,000 launch plan, set intentionally below market because the team is fractional and the project is early. They may change.

Founder role

The Founder, CEO & Executive Technical Director role is held by the project founder. It covers strategy, fundraising, the website and cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, open-data systems, and coordination of contractors and vendors. The founder does not hold scientific sign-off: that belongs to the Principal Investigator. Because the founder's salary is a conflict of interest, it is to be approved by disinterested board members, documented in board minutes, and tied to maintaining at least six months of projected operating runway.

Planned hiring order

  1. Days 1 to 30: Principal Investigator and Metrology Engineer.
  2. Days 15 to 45: legal counsel, Bookkeeper, and Community & Grants Lead.
  3. Before laboratory contracting: Research Operations Coordinator.
  4. Before data collection: independent Statistician, with the analysis plan preregistered first.
  5. After a larger raise: consider full-time positions.

Services procured, not hired

Securities and token counsel, entity formation, smart-contract security review, and a host university or commercial laboratory are contracted services rather than staff. Advisory board members are initially volunteers, with approved expenses reimbursed.

Independence and conflicts

Statistical and methods reviewers must be independent of the people who run the experiment. All applicants must declare financial or intellectual conflicts. Pay does not depend on the experiment producing a positive result, and contributions or compensation do not purchase equity, tokens, royalties, or a financial return.
