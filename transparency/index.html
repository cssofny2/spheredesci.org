Project / Stewardship

A visible plan for every dollar.

The first year is scoped around protocol review, measurement-system qualification, a controlled pilot, and public reporting. Phase 1 covers that first year; Phases 2 and 3 are described below. A budget is a planning estimate, not evidence that funds have been raised.

Updated October 14, 2026 · Proposal stage

Funding readiness

Entity formed; pre-funding. SPHERE Research, LLC was incorporated in Delaware on October 23, 2026 (File No. 2358393; EIN 43-2602592). No public payment collection, fundraising campaign, or treasury balance is active. The site currently accepts expressions of interest only. No contribution is represented as tax-deductible.

Three funding phases

The project plans to raise money in three phases. Each phase opens only after the one before it is complete, and its scope and amount are published before it opens. These are proposals. No phase has opened, and no funds have been raised. All amounts are planning estimates, not vendor quotes.

  • Phase 1, about $86,000. This matches the Standard column of the draft budget above: independent review, measurement-system qualification, the blinded pilot and public reporting, with rented or partner-lab instrument access.
  • Phase 2, $450,000 to $1.2 million. The next step after Phase 1: opening and equipping a dedicated lab, buying or renting more instruments, and hiring experts. The final scope and amount will be published before it opens.
  • Phase 3, about $2.1 million in total. The top end of the budget. This is the cumulative total across all three phases, not a separate raise. It covers the full lab, equipment and team to run the complete set of experiments and publish the results. The final scope and amount will be published before it opens. Dedicated lab rent and salaried staff are not in the Phase 1 budget.

Funding follows readiness

  1. G0

    Founding packet

    Draft published; adoption and independent feedback remain outstanding.

  2. G1

    Protocol review

    Two independent reviewers and a statistician review the final experiment plan.

  3. G2

    Measurement qualification

    Calibration establishes uncertainty, stability, and detectable-effect bounds.

  4. G3

    Preregistration

    The confirmatory design and analysis are frozen before data collection.

  5. G4

    Pilot and reporting

    The blinded study and its analysis are released, including null or inconclusive outcomes.

  6. G5

    Independent replication

    An independent group tests whether any candidate signal survives repetition.

Financial and governance commitments

  • Dedicated project accounts and bookkeeping before payment collection.
  • Documented founder loans or capital contributions, separate from donor funds.
  • Written quotes for equipment purchases above $1,000.
  • Quarterly summaries of receipts, spending, commitments, and milestone progress.
  • Disclosure of sponsor conflicts, with no sponsor control of scientific conclusions.
  • Founder retains legal, financial, and safety accountability during the founding phase; community decisions are advisory.

These are proposed operating commitments, not a claim that a bank account or bookkeeping system already exists. The governance review triggers are 25 active contributors, a treasury exceeding $50,000, or 12 months, whichever applicable milestone comes first.

Where financial reports will appear

No financial report is available yet. Once the operating entity starts receiving project funds, this section will link dated reports and explain restricted funds, outstanding commitments, and changes to the plan. Private donor details and account credentials will never be included in public reporting.
