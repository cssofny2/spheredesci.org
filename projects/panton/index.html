What We Do

Services Built Around Your Property

From dependable weekly lawn stripes to comprehensive bed maintenance and mulch installation, Panton Bros, LLC treats your yard with top-tier care.

Lawn Mowing & Trim

Consistent, crisp cutting with sharp blades on a regular weekly schedule. Includes line trimming around edges, fences, and obstacles plus blowing off driveways.

  • Crisp lawn striping & uniform cut
  • String trimming & perimeter edging
  • Hard surface blowing & cleanup
Spring & Fall Cleanups

Thorough seasonal property maintenance to prep lawn beds for spring growth or clean up heavy leaf accumulation before Rochester winters hit.

  • Complete leaf & twig removal
  • Garden bed blowout & clearing
  • Perennial cutting & final seasonal cut
Mulch & Bed Design

Elevate your home's curb appeal with deep shovel-cut bed edging, premium triple-shredded dark mulch installation, shrub trimming, and perennial plantings.

  • Deep spade edging for bed definition
  • Premium black, brown, or red mulch
  • Shrub pruning & ornamental planting
Aeration & Overseeding

Relieve soil compaction and fill in thin patches with core lawn aeration paired with premium cool-season grass seed formulated for Rochester weather.

  • Core soil aeration for nutrient flow
  • High-grade Rochester lawn seed blend
  • Thicket patch repair & dethatching
