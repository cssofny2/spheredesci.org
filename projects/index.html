How these pages relate to the research

Project S.P.H.E.R.E. is a research project. It plans a careful lab test of whether moving an object changes its resonant frequency. The pages in this section are not part of that work.

Nothing on a project page is a research finding. The research has no results yet. A listing here is not an endorsement, a partnership or a statement of shared ownership. Each project page is responsible for its own content, prices and promises.

Check before you rely on a page. For the research itself, use only the pages linked below. Contact details for a listed project appear on that project’s own page.
