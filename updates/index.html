November 24, 2026: Organized site navigation, hand-drawn icons, and homepage sections

Posted November 24, 2026

Reorganized the site navigation across desktop and mobile into four clear sections — Experiment, Library, Governance, and Community — alongside direct links to the Visual Lab (SPHEREVL) and Search. Added 29 custom hand-drawn scientific-etching SVG icons in /assets/nav-icons.svg so each section and destination has a distinct visual emblem rather than text-only links. Restructured the homepage into seven numbered chapters with a sticky horizontal wayfinder bar and an Explore by section directory. This is a website interface and information-architecture upgrade; no research claims, protocols, or results changed.

November 24, 2026: Routed lab subdomain to live Control Room dashboard

Posted November 24, 2026

Configured routing on lab.spheredesci.org to point directly to the live Control Room simulator dashboard on Vercel. SPHEREVL remains an educational simulation; it contains no physical measurements or scientific validation.

November 4, 2026: Clarified homepage analysis wording

Updated the homepage's preregistered-analysis summary to identify registered Protocol LOC-1 as the authority for the plan, without asserting a smallest effect of interest, an alpha level, or an equivalence test that could not be verified directly against the OSF record for this update. Those three details remain unverified here; the registration remains the source of truth.

November 4, 2026: Q factor explainer

Added Q factor (quality factor) of a resonator, explained to Learn. It defines Q, shows how it relates to resonance bandwidth, damping ratio and ring-down time, works through an illustrative calculation, lists what lowers Q in real resonators, and compares three ways to measure it. The page is an educational explainer; it reports no measurements and does not say which quantities the registered LOC-1 protocol records.

November 3, 2026: Environment record-and-replay protocol note

Added Recording a Copper Sphere's Resonance at One Location and Replaying the Environment at Another to the research journal. It separates the sphere's intrinsic resonance from the environment a site delivers to it, and proposes isolating the sphere at a second location and replaying the first location's recorded vibration and magnetic field under closed-loop control, with sham and self-replay controls. The page is a proposal-stage engineering note; it does not amend the OSF registration and reports no measurements or results.

November 1, 2026: Blind analysis research note

Added Why LOC-1 hides the position labels: blind analysis in precision measurement to the research journal. It explains why the registered plan gives the placement key to an independent position keeper, surveys how blinding has been used in physics, including hidden offsets and blind signal injections, and notes what blinding cannot do: it does not remove physical effects such as temperature that are correlated with position. The page is an educational design note; it does not amend the OSF registration and reports no measurements or results.

October 28, 2026: Homepage video plays on phones

Posted October 28, 2026

Fixed the homepage explainer video so it plays on iPhones and Android phones. The video was re-encoded in a format phone hardware can decode, and the site now delivers video in the byte ranges that iOS Safari requires. The content, captions and transcript are unchanged. This is a website fix; it reports no measurements or research results.

October 23, 2026: Protocol LOC-1 registered and live

Posted October 23, 2026

Published the official Protocol LOC-1 preregistration on OSF and archived the technical whitepaper on Zenodo (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23583932). The sampling plan, variable definitions, and analysis specifications are finalized in the registered plan. The registration documents the planned study; it does not report completed measurements or findings.

October 22, 2026: Whitepaper PDF and academic indexing

Posted October 22, 2026

Improved the whitepaper’s academic indexing metadata and author attribution, normalized the displayed copper material constants, removed expired private source links, and added a print-to-PDF action with print-specific table and page-break styling. The PDF action opens the browser’s print dialog; no research results or new measurements are reported.

October 21, 2026: Research publication platform

Posted October 21, 2026

Expanded the build-time MDX publishing platform with generated category, tag, archive, and author routes; reusable scientific content components; citation and article-reference linking; author pages; and article-level SEO metadata. Research pages now include mobile-aware table-of-contents navigation, sharing, author attribution, related discovery, and a proposal-stage disclaimer. Search ranks and highlights matches across article text, authors, categories, and tags, with keyboard navigation. Production analytics add daily aggregated reading-time and coarse scroll-depth metrics without visitor identifiers. Contributor guides document content, author profiles, SEO, and deployment. The project remains pre-launch and reports no measurements or research results.

October 16, 2026: Resonance guides and verification protocol

Posted October 16, 2026

Expanded the resonance topic with five new guides in Learn: can sound change an object’s resonant frequency?, how to measure a sphere’s resonant frequency and mode shapes, how to shift a resonant frequency, impulse response measurement and sine sweeps and a modal analysis glossary. Added a proposed A/B/A excitation-off verification protocol to the research pages, and revised the record-and-replay article with quick answers, a section on why one microphone recording is not the sphere’s state, and four levels of reproduction. These are explainers and proposals. They report no measurements or results.

October 16, 2026: Record-and-replay resonance article

Posted October 16, 2026

Added Record-and-replay resonance: can playback retune a copper sphere? to the research pages. It reviews whether recording a hollow copper sphere’s resonance near 230 kHz and replaying it could change the sphere’s natural frequency, what mechanisms could move a mode, a frequency-budget calculator, controls and a proposed test sequence. The page is a proposal and a request for comment from acousticians, resonant-ultrasound specialists and control engineers. It contains no measurements or results, and responses go to contact@spheredesci.org.

October 16, 2026: Hollow copper sphere reference page

Posted October 16, 2026

Added a hollow copper sphere page to the resources library. It explains why the reference artifact is a thin-walled copper shell, the copper purity it needs, a proposed manufacturing route, a provisional size, mass and density, and an unquoted planning cost range. These are design estimates for review. They are not specifications, supplier quotations or results, and the final geometry will come from finite-element modelling.

October 16, 2026: Community votes

Posted October 16, 2026

Added a Community votes section where visitors can keep or drop items, rate them from 1 to 10 and leave comments, with one ballot per item per browser. The first vote is the logo concepts: 109 marks from six design rounds, plus a remix lab where a visitor can describe a change in words and enter the edited mark into the vote. Each concept is shown as a full lockup with its S.P.H.E.R.E. wordmark and tagline. These are informal preference polls about the project’s public identity. They are not research and do not measure or validate anything about the experiment. The privacy notice describes what the polls store.

October 15, 2026: Projects section and Panton Bros page

Posted October 15, 2026

Added a Projects section for pages hosted here that are not part of the research, and its first entry, a Panton Bros, LLC page. It is a separate small-business page, unrelated to the research. Its request form opens the visitor’s email app; nothing is collected or stored by this site.

October 9, 2026: synchronized-measurement research added

Posted October 9, 2026, 9:49 PM EDT

Eight new pages and several additions to existing pages extend the measurement discussion around LOC-1. They are proposals for public review, not results.

  • Synchronized measurement research hub: entry point for the new material.
  • Synchronized four-object frequency measurement protocol: a proposed amendment for concurrent readout, common clocking and blinded position trials. It does not replace the registered LOC-1 protocol.
  • Frequency measurement error budget: timing, drift and false positives: how to convert timing, drift and temperature effects into an uncertainty register.
  • Does Earth’s motion affect resonance measurements?, What a miniature clock network teaches SPHERE about synchronized measurements and Earth rotation and resonator tests: what SPHERE can learn: reference-frame questions and original commentary on published related work.
  • Hardware for synchronized resonance measurements: hardware categories and a procurement checklist, without vendor photographs because image rights are not cleared.
  • Community review: how would you improve the experiment?: review prompts and the routes available today for sending critique.

Existing pages updated: the homepage (research library, LOC-1 section and questions), protocol (proposed-amendment summary), resources, the equipment plan, collaboration page, FAQ, site navigation and the sitemap.

These remain documentation milestones. No measurements, community responses, preregistration, installed hardware or funding changes are represented. Independent review of the four-object proposal has not occurred.

October 2, 2026: public research foundation

The public description and research charter were added to the website, and the full project name was standardized to Spatial Positioning Harmonic Empirical Resonance Experiment. Homepage language was revised to distinguish the hypothesis from established findings.

The research library now includes a draft LOC-1 protocol summary, a first-year budget and transparency page, FAQs, collaboration guidance, and this update log. The site no longer displays placeholder funding totals, wallet counts, or token-sale claims.

These are documentation milestones only. Entity formation, advisor engagement, instrument qualification, preregistration, data collection, and replication remain uncompleted or unconfirmed.

Correction, October 23, 2026: Protocol LOC-1 was registered on OSF after this update was published. The original wording above is retained as a historical record.

Next on the research roadmap

  1. Adopt the founding packet and confirm the operating entity.
  2. Recruit independent reviewers and obtain instrument-access quotes.
  3. Validate sphere dimensions, selected modes, and the proposed acquisition chain.
  4. Review the proposed synchronized four-object amendment and decide whether to adopt, revise or drop it.
  5. Build the calibration and repeatability dataset needed for an uncertainty budget.
  6. Run the preregistered LOC-1 plan when technical readiness, equipment, and funding permit.
  7. Publish all outcomes and prepare an independently usable replication package.

No calendar date is promised for the first run. LOC-1 is preregistered; data collection still depends on technical readiness, equipment access, and funding.
