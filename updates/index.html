Project / Field notes

Progress, with the unfinished work visible.

Dated notes record what has changed and what still needs to happen. Publishing a website or protocol draft is not the same as completing a research gate.

Updated October 9, 2026 · Proposal stage

October 9, 2026 (late evening): SPHEREVL visual lab page overhauled and promoted

Posted October 9, 2026, 10:41 PM EDT

The visual lab page was rebuilt and is now linked from the top of the homepage, the main navigation and the footer.

  • Real screenshots of the Control Room simulator (lab overview, 3D chamber, VNA and laser-vibrometer panels) and original illustration art.
  • Repository facts from the public application repository: 26 commits (October 3 to 8, 2026), technology stack, tests and CI, and a complete linked development history.
  • Stated limitations, run-it-yourself steps and an expanded Q&A. The page remains an educational simulation; it is not physical measurement or validation.

October 9, 2026 (evening): directory, links, newsletter, calendar and applications

Posted October 9, 2026, 10:13 PM EDT

New sections make the project easier to follow and join. They add structure only; no event, social profile or other party is listed until it is confirmed.

  • Links and profiles: official channels. Profiles are listed only once their URLs are supplied.
  • Directory: active parties, listed with permission.
  • Calendar: upcoming events and dates, with a downloadable .ics file. No events are scheduled yet.
  • Newsletter: subscribe, opt out, latest issue and a permanent archive, starting with Issue 1.
  • Candidate application: professional details, accomplishments, references and résumé upload.
  • The privacy notice now covers applications, the newsletter and the directory.

October 9, 2026: synchronized-measurement research added

Posted October 9, 2026, 9:49 PM EDT

Eight new pages and several additions to existing pages extend the measurement discussion around LOC-1. They are proposals for public review, not results.

  • Synchronized measurement research hub: entry point for the new material.
  • Synchronized four-object frequency measurement protocol: a proposed amendment for concurrent readout, common clocking and blinded position trials. It does not replace the LOC-1 draft.
  • Frequency measurement error budget: timing, drift and false positives: how to convert timing, drift and temperature effects into an uncertainty register.
  • Does Earth’s motion affect resonance measurements?, What a miniature clock network teaches SPHERE about synchronized measurements and Earth rotation and resonator tests: what SPHERE can learn: reference-frame questions and original commentary on published related work.
  • Hardware for synchronized resonance measurements: hardware categories and a procurement checklist, without vendor photographs because image rights are not cleared.
  • Community review: how would you improve the experiment?: review prompts and the routes available today for sending critique.

Existing pages updated: the homepage (research library, LOC-1 section and questions), protocol (proposed-amendment summary), resources, the equipment plan, collaboration page, FAQ, site navigation and the sitemap.

These remain documentation milestones. No measurements, community responses, preregistration, installed hardware or funding changes are represented. Independent review of the four-object proposal has not occurred.

October 2, 2026: public research foundation

The public description and research charter were added to the website, and the full project name was standardized to Spatial Positioning Harmonic Empirical Resonance Experiment. Homepage language was revised to distinguish the hypothesis from established findings.

The research library now includes a draft LOC-1 protocol summary, a first-year budget and transparency page, FAQs, collaboration guidance, and this update log. The site no longer displays placeholder funding totals, wallet counts, or token-sale claims.

These are documentation milestones only. Entity formation, advisor engagement, instrument qualification, preregistration, data collection, and replication remain uncompleted or unconfirmed.

Next on the research roadmap

  1. Adopt the founding packet and confirm the operating entity.
  2. Recruit independent reviewers and obtain instrument-access quotes.
  3. Validate sphere dimensions, selected modes, and the proposed acquisition chain.
  4. Review the proposed synchronized four-object amendment and decide whether to adopt, revise or drop it.
  5. Build the calibration and repeatability dataset needed for an uncertainty budget.
  6. Complete and preregister the confirmatory design before LOC-1 runs.
  7. Publish all outcomes and prepare an independently usable replication package.

No calendar date is promised for the first run. Progress depends on technical feasibility, review, equipment access, and funding readiness.

How to follow changes

Major public updates are posted here. The GitHub commit history provides the detailed website change record. For important protocol updates, the project will record the version, reason, and whether the change preceded or followed preregistration.
