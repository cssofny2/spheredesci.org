Issue 1 · October 9, 2026

Field Notes, Issue 1: synchronized measurement, and a way to join in

Welcome to the first S.P.H.E.R.E. newsletter. It records what changed, what did not, and where you can help. Nothing here is a research result: the project is at proposal stage and no measurements have been collected.

What is new

  • Synchronized-measurement research. Eight pages were added on October 9, 2026, including a proposed four-object protocol, an error budget, an article on Earth's motion and resonance measurements, and a hardware guide. Start at the research hub.
  • Still the same LOC-1 draft. The four-object design is a proposal for review and does not amend the current LOC-1 protocol.
  • Ways to take part. A community design review is open, a candidate application form accepts professional details, references and a résumé, and this newsletter now has subscribe and opt-out forms.
  • Directory, links and calendar. The directory, links and profiles and calendar pages are new. No events are scheduled yet.

What has not changed

  • No preregistration, data collection, installed hardware or funding total exists.
  • Contributions do not purchase equity, tokens, royalties or a financial return.

How you can help

Critique the four-object proposal, offer instrument access or review expertise on the collaboration page, or see the roles the project hopes to fill if funded.

You are receiving this issue because it is posted on the site. To change your subscription at any time, use the forms on the newsletter page.
