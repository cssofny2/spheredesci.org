What happens when you register interest.

This notice describes the current website form and its delivery route. It is not a claim that a future legal entity has already been formed.

Updated October 21, 2026 · Proposal stage

Information you choose to submit

The interest form asks for your name or alias, email address, selected role, and an optional message. It is used to respond to your inquiry and provide the protocol, review, and funding updates described on the form. Registration is not a purchase, investment, or grant of membership rights.

Do not submit credentials, seed phrases, sensitive medical information, or confidential third-party material. The form does not request a crypto wallet address.

Delivery and third-party services

The form posts to FormSubmit and is configured to deliver submissions to contact@spheredesci.org, with an autoresponse to the submitted email. Third-party service availability and email delivery can vary; a delivery configuration is not a guarantee that every message reaches an inbox.

The website is hosted through Cloudflare. It also loads fonts from Google Fonts and icon styling from cdnjs. Those providers may process technical connection information according to their own policies.

Browser storage and publication

The website does not store your submitted email in browser storage or build a browser-side analytics profile. The confirmation page does not repeat your email address. Submitted information still passes through the form provider and the project mailbox.

Form submissions are not published as research data. Names, endorsements, and affiliations must not be posted publicly without permission. Public GitHub issues are different: information posted there is visible to others, so use email for private requests.

Aggregate website analytics

On the production site, first-party analytics counts selected research, knowledge, and glossary page views, aggregates search terms, and records referring domains or same-site paths by day. It also stores daily per-page totals for active reading duration (capped at one hour per visit) and scroll depth rounded to 25-percentage-point increments. It also counts daily clicks on whitepaper, repository, SPHEREVL and outbound links (recording only the destination path or hostname, never the full URL or query string) and FAQ openings per page. These are aggregate counts, not individual reading histories. It does not use an analytics cookie, store IP addresses or visitor identifiers, or retain individual event records. Email addresses and URL strings are redacted from search terms before aggregation; please do not enter personal or sensitive information in search. Only aggregate totals and normalized search terms are retained, and records are deleted after 90 days. The browser analytics script honors Do Not Track. Analytics is disabled for local and preview deployments.

Cloudflare may process technical connection information to provide hosting and security, subject to its own policies. That provider-side processing is separate from the aggregate analytics dataset described here.

Career applications New · Oct 9, 2026

The candidate application asks for your name, email, optional phone, location, professional history, skills, accomplishments, the names and contact details of references, a résumé file, and a conflict-of-interest statement. It is used to consider you for a role or reviewer position.

The form posts to FormSubmit and is delivered to contact@spheredesci.org, with a copy of your answers sent to the email you provide. The résumé travels as an email attachment. According to FormSubmit's documentation, uploaded files are not kept in its submission archive and other submission data is retained for a limited period; the project mailbox keeps what it receives until you ask for removal, because no retention schedule has been adopted.

Applications are not published and references are not contacted without further notice to you. You must have each reference's permission before listing them. Do not submit government ID numbers, bank details, date of birth, health information or confidential employer or client material.

Community votes New · Oct 16, 2026

The community votes store your ballot (Keep or Drop, a rating from 1 to 10, and an optional comment) and any remix you submit (the drawing, its name and the text prompt you wrote). They do not ask for your name or email.

To keep one ballot per item per browser, the site sets a cookie named sv holding a random voter token. It is HttpOnly, lasts one year, and is used only by the voting pages. The database stores a SHA-256 hash of the token, not the token. For daily abuse limits it also stores a salted hash of your network address, not the address itself. Comments, remix names and prompts are shown publicly on the results page, so do not put personal information in them. Links in comments are rejected.

When you use the AI helper in the remix lab, your prompt and the mark you are editing are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to produce the edited drawing. Ballots and remixes are kept until you ask for removal: email contact@spheredesci.org with the name of the item and roughly when you voted. Because votes are anonymous, we can remove a ballot only if you can identify it. Clearing your cookies breaks the link between your browser and your past ballots.

Newsletter New · Oct 9, 2026

The newsletter page collects an email address (and optionally a name and topic interests) with your consent. Requests are sent through FormSubmit to the project mailbox and handled by a person; no email-marketing platform is used yet. You can opt out with the form on that page or by emailing contact@spheredesci.org. Published issues remain on the site as an archive and do not contain subscriber information.

Directory and links New · Oct 9, 2026

The directory lists people or organizations only with their permission, and does not publish personal phone numbers or home addresses. Ask to be corrected or removed at any time. The links page contains outbound links to third-party sites that have their own privacy practices.

Privacy questions and removal requests

Contact contact@spheredesci.org to request correction, removal of your submitted contact information, or cessation of project communications. Identify the email used for the form so the request can be matched; do not send extra identity documents unless specifically required.

A formal retention schedule and entity-specific policy remain to be adopted. This notice will be revised when the legal operator, form provider, storage practices, or communication workflow changes.
